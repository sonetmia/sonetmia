<p align="justify">
Md. Sonet Mia is an English graduate, educator, writer, and digital creator from Tangail, Bangladesh. He is a Graphic Designer &amp; Vector Illustrator, AI Tool Builder, and Founder of the <a href="https://sonet-ecosystem.vercel.app">SONET Ecosystem</a>.
</p>

<p align="justify">
He builds AI tools for creators — image-to-prompt, image upscaler, stock AI, and prompt studios — and sells vector art on Adobe Stock. He teaches microstock as Trainer &amp; Team Leader of 100+ students.
</p>

</p>
